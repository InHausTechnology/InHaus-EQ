InHaus EQ: How to Install
=========================

1. Drag "InHaus EQ" onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Open InHaus EQ from your Applications folder.
   macOS will say it can't verify the app. That's because InHaus EQ comes
   from an independent developer and isn't notarized by Apple. Click "Done".

3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security,
   and click "Open Anyway" next to InHaus EQ. Confirm with your password
   or Touch ID. You only have to do this once.

4. When InHaus EQ asks to record system audio, click "Allow". That's how
   the EQ reaches your audio. Nothing is recorded or saved.

InHaus EQ lives in your menu bar (top right of your screen), not the Dock.

Requires macOS 14.2 (Sonoma) or later.
